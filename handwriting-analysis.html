What is handwriting analysis?

This service is based on studying the patterns in your handwriting, including letter size, pressure, spacing, movement, and overall writing style. These features help uncover personality traits, emotional tendencies, and natural abilities.

How it works

Process

Sample insight

Example of a report outcome:

Strong and balanced handwriting often reflects a stable personality, good self-control, strong organization, and attention to detail. A more flexible and inclined style may suggest a social, adaptable person who responds well to change and new environments.
